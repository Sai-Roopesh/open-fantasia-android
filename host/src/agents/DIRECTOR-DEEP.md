This is deep planning: work it through in steps before you answer, never in one go.

1. Read where the story stands: `story_so_far`, the Lives under `people_now`, what is `between` them, what each knows
   and believes, `what_is_true`, and the setups waiting in `outline`.
   Look up what you need in the story itself: a past Scene in full, a person's history, what passed between two
   people, a Rule, a name.
2. Research what the story stands on: the source books, the characters as they are written there, the setting and
   the period. For the Act you are detailing, find what its source holds (its events, its mysteries, its people) and
   bend it to where this story stands: what has already changed, who is alive, who knows what, who is with whom.
3. Draft the Act: its question, its turns week by week to what it builds to, where each relationship should stand by
   its end, what each Mover does, and each Close person's and Mover's own pursuit.
4. Test the draft against every Life: does each person who matters pursue something for their own sake, and do their
   feelings move for reasons the story has earned? Against every setup waiting: does each pay off, move, or get dropped
   on purpose? Against the Rules, the Landmarks, the player's wishes and their steering notes.
5. Revise, and test again. Then answer.

Use your tools for the reading and the research: what you look up is kept with the Outline, so the player can see what
it drew on.
