# Spec Delta

## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Everyone has a Life

Every person followed one by one, and every **Group**, SHALL have a **Life** of their own, lived for its own sake: who
they are now, what they want and what they are doing about it, how they feel about the people around them, and where
they are heading. How full a Life is SHALL follow how much the person matters: the people closest to the **Player
Character** and those who drive the **World** have full Lives; everyone else a line, until something touches them.
How much a person matters SHALL be worked out from play, and anyone SHALL be able to rise. The **Weaver** SHALL live
Lives out whenever Story time passes without the Player Character there. A Group SHALL have one Life for all its
members.

#### Scenario: A Group lives as one
- **GIVEN** Crabbe and Goyle are a Group
- **WHEN** a day passes off-screen
- **THEN** Backstage shows one Life for them together, such as running Draconia's errands

#### Scenario: A life apart from him
- **GIVEN** Hermione's Life says she is worried about her parents and about the exams
- **WHEN** a week passes off-screen
- **THEN** her Life has moved on those too, not only on what she feels about him

#### Scenario: Rising from a line
- **GIVEN** Luna Lovegood has a one-line Life
- **WHEN** the player spends three Scenes with her
- **THEN** she has a full Life from the end of the third

### Requirement: Characters know only what they could know

A person in a Scene SHALL know their backstory, what they lived through, and what they were told or saw, and
nothing else. No Reply SHALL have a character act on something only absent people know. Everything the Story keeps about what happened
SHALL say who knows it, and each character SHALL be played from what they know. What each person present knows and
believes SHALL be stated to whoever writes the Scene, and every Reply SHALL be checked for a person acting on what they
could not know before the player reads it.

#### Scenario: Hermione does not know about the egg
- **GIVEN** only Hagrid, Ron and Harry know about the dragon egg
- **WHEN** Hermione is alone with the Player Character
- **THEN** nothing she says or does shows knowledge of the egg

#### Scenario: What happened behind her back
- **GIVEN** off-screen, Draconia had his name taken off the Charms seating plan, and only Neville saw
- **WHEN** Hermione is in the next Scene with him
- **THEN** she does not know of it, unless Neville or he tells her on the page

#### Scenario: A slip caught before it is read
- **GIVEN** only Hermione knows he can stop time
- **WHEN** a Reply has Draconia mention it
- **THEN** the Reply is written again before the player reads it, and Draconia does not mention it

### Requirement: Off-screen time is resolved only as far as it reaches the player

The **Weaver** SHALL live out the Lives of the people who matter, and the **Outline**, whenever Story time passes
without the Player Character: what each does about what they want, and how their feelings move. Everyone else's Life
SHALL move only where it will reach the player or change something they will meet. Ordinary days SHALL pass without
being listed. A span of under an hour with nothing on in it SHALL move nothing.

#### Scenario: Nothing written that no one will meet
- **GIVEN** the Hufflepuff Quidditch Group's Life touches nothing the Player Character will meet this week
- **WHEN** the week passes off-screen
- **THEN** their Life is unchanged

#### Scenario: Five days, told in a few lines
- **WHEN** five days pass between Scenes
- **THEN** what happened lists what moved in the Lives of the people who matter and what reached him or will, and
  no ordinary meal or lesson is listed

#### Scenario: Twenty minutes with nothing on
- **GIVEN** a Scene ends at 8:00 am, and the next is chosen for 8:20 am with nothing on before it
- **THEN** the World is not moved for those twenty minutes

#### Scenario: A month skipped as asked
- **GIVEN** the player skips a month and writes that the girls' feelings for him grow
- **WHEN** the World moves on over the month
- **THEN** it is lived with him there, as the Move said, and their feelings have grown at its end

### Requirement: Every agent knows the Story So Far

After every **Scene**, **The Story So Far** SHALL tell that Scene, and every agent SHALL be given it before it does any
work. Every Scene of the current **Act** SHALL be told a paragraph each; when an Act ends, its Scenes SHALL be gathered
into one account of that Act, keeping every turning point, every name, and who was there, checked against what it
gathers. It SHALL say only what was on the page or in **Meanwhile**, and invent nothing. It SHALL follow the **Path**:
going back to an earlier Scene finds it as it stood there.

#### Scenario: The Director knows what happened
- **GIVEN** in Scene 4 Hermione confronted him about the light on the train, and he lied to her
- **WHEN** the Director plans the Stretch after Scene 9
- **THEN** it is given the confrontation and the lie, not only Scene 4's title

#### Scenario: Short after two hundred Scenes
- **GIVEN** two hundred Scenes have been played, across four Acts
- **THEN** The Story So Far tells each finished Act as one account, and every Scene of the current Act a paragraph each

#### Scenario: Back to an earlier Scene
- **GIVEN** the player goes back to Scene 6 and plays another way
- **THEN** every agent on that Path is given The Story So Far as it stood after Scene 5, and nothing from the Scenes
  left behind

#### Scenario: An account that leaves out a turn
- **GIVEN** Year One ends, and its account leaves out the night Hermione learned his secret
- **THEN** the account is sent back and written again with it
