# Spec Delta

## Purpose

Gives every Story a direction to its end, woven from everyone's Lives, so that every Stretch builds toward something
and the Story follows wherever the player takes it.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Every Story has an Outline to its end

Every Story SHALL have an **Outline** from now to its end, in **Acts**: the current Act in detail (its question, the
turns along the way roughly week by week, where each main relationship should stand by its end, what each person who
drives the **World** is doing, and every setup with its payoff), the next Act outlined, and the rest sketched to how
the Story ends. The **Director** SHALL write it when the Story starts.

#### Scenario: The first Outline
- **GIVEN** The Malfoy Problem is imported
- **WHEN** the player opens Backstage before the first Scene ends
- **THEN** it shows Year One in detail, building to the Philosopher's Stone, with where Draconia and Hermione should
  stand by June; Year Two outlined; and the years after sketched to the end of the Story

#### Scenario: A small Story's Outline
- **GIVEN** The Night Train to Kalka is imported
- **THEN** its Outline is one Act, the night, with where it is heading by morning

### Requirement: The Outline is woven from Lives

Each person who matters SHALL have intentions in the Outline for the current Act, for their own sake and not only
about the **Player Character**: what they pursue, how their feelings about the people around them move, and where
they are heading by the Act's end. The Player Character's story SHALL be where these **Lives** cross his.

#### Scenario: Draconia's own year
- **GIVEN** The Malfoy Problem's Outline for Year One
- **THEN** it says what Draconia pursues this year apart from Roopesh, such as her father's plans for her and the
  Seeker's place, and how she comes to feel about Pansy and Hermione

### Requirement: Nothing enters without a payoff

A new person, threat, mystery or place SHALL enter the Story only as a setup the Outline means to pay off, and the
Outline SHALL keep every setup until it is paid off or deliberately dropped.

#### Scenario: A stranger with a purpose
- **GIVEN** the Director brings a stranger into Hogsmeade asking about the train
- **THEN** the Outline says what the stranger sets up and in which turn of the Act it pays off

#### Scenario: A setup kept
- **GIVEN** nine dementors went missing in Scene 12
- **WHEN** later Stretches are planned
- **THEN** the Outline keeps the missing dementors until they pay off or the Director drops them, and says which

### Requirement: The Outline is reconsidered after every Scene

At the end of every **Scene**, and never between the exchanges of one, the Director SHALL reconsider the Outline in
the light of how the Scene went: a focused revision by default, and a deep one when the Scene turned the Story, an
Act ended, or the player steered. The Outline SHALL follow wherever the player takes the Story.

#### Scenario: An ordinary Scene
- **GIVEN** a breakfast Scene in which nothing turned
- **WHEN** it ends
- **THEN** the Director adjusts the current Act where the Scene touched it, and does not re-plan it deeply

#### Scenario: A Scene that turns the Story
- **GIVEN** Year One's Outline needs Lucius Malfoy in June
- **WHEN** a Scene ends in which the player kills Lucius
- **THEN** the Director re-plans Year One deeply before the Day View opens, and the Scene's end says it is
  rethinking Year One

#### Scenario: Not between exchanges
- **WHEN** a Move inside a Scene is answered
- **THEN** the Outline is unchanged until the Scene ends

#### Scenario: Someone grows close
- **GIVEN** the player has spent three Scenes with Luna Lovegood
- **WHEN** the third ends
- **THEN** Luna has a full Life and a place in the current Act's turns

### Requirement: Each Stretch is the next step of the current Act

Each **Stretch** SHALL be planned as the next step of the current Act: every Scene that matters in it SHALL serve a
turn of the Act or a person's Life, and arrange where their Lives cross the Player Character's.

#### Scenario: No villain from nowhere
- **WHEN** the Director plans a Stretch
- **THEN** every Scene that matters in it names the turn of the Act or the Life it serves, and none brings in a threat
  the Outline does not mean to pay off

### Requirement: The Director plans by working the plan through

Writing an Act in detail SHALL be worked through, not answered in one go: the Director SHALL read the Story, research
the source material and the setting, draft, test the draft against everyone's Lives and the setups already planted,
and revise. What it looked up SHALL be kept with the plan.

#### Scenario: Year Two from the books
- **WHEN** Year One ends and the Director plans Year Two in detail
- **THEN** its plan draws on what the second book holds, bent to where this Story stands, and Backstage can show
  what it looked up

### Requirement: The player sees and steers the Outline

Backstage SHALL show the Outline: the current Act's question and turns, where each relationship is heading, the
setups waiting to pay off, and the Acts after it. The player SHALL be able to write steering notes there; the Director
SHALL treat a note as an order, re-plan deeply at the next Scene's end, and follow the note over its own reading of
play.

#### Scenario: Steering a year's end
- **WHEN** the player writes the note "Year One ends with Draconia breaking from her father"
- **THEN** after the next Scene's end, Year One in the Outline builds toward that break

#### Scenario: A note over play
- **GIVEN** play has drawn Hermione to the centre of the Act
- **WHEN** the player writes "keep Hermione a rival this year"
- **THEN** the next Outline keeps her a rival for the rest of Year One
