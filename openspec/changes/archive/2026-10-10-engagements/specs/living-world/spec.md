# Spec Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: An Engagement is recorded where it is made

An **Engagement** SHALL be recorded when it is made: on the page of a **Scene**, in the words of a **Move** or a
**Reply**, or off-screen, in a line of **Meanwhile** that reaches the **Player Character**. It SHALL say when and
where, who will be there, whether he is expected, and whether it repeats each week. One made on the page SHALL be
recorded only with the words it was made in, quoted as they were written, so that no Engagement is invented. A
**Scene** in which an Engagement is moved or called off, in front of him, SHALL record that too. Only what the Player
Character knows SHALL be an Engagement: an arrangement made where he could not hear it is not his.

#### Scenario: Made in a Reply
- **WHEN** a Scene ends in which Draconia says "Wednesdays, in the library, half past seven. You'll be there before
  me." and the player's Move answers "yeah alright i will be there"
- **THEN** a weekly Engagement is recorded for Wednesdays at 7:30 pm in the library, with Draconia, he is expected,
  and it quotes her words and his

#### Scenario: Words that are not on the page
- **WHEN** the record of a Scene gives an Engagement whose quote is not in any Move or Reply of that Scene
- **THEN** that Engagement is left out and noted, and the rest of the record is kept

#### Scenario: Arranged behind his back
- **WHEN** Pansy and Draconia agree, in the Slytherin common room, to meet at the Owlery at six
- **THEN** no Engagement of his is recorded
