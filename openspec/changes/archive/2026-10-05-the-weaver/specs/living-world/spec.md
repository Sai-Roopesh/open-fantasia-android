# Spec Delta

## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Only the player makes facts about the Player Character

What the **Player Character** said, did, thought or felt SHALL be what the player's Moves and edits say, and nothing
else. A Reply that has someone recall or claim something about the Player Character that no Move said SHALL stay a
line of that Reply: it SHALL never be remembered as something that happened, and no later Scene SHALL treat it as
true. When the **World** moves on, it SHALL move on around the Player Character: what happened in a Scene happened
when the Scene says, and nothing has him anywhere, doing anything or knowing anything off-screen that no Move said.

#### Scenario: An invented past is not remembered
- **GIVEN** a Reply had Hermione say "you told me at breakfast you hate flying", and no Move ever said so
- **WHEN** a later Scene brings up flying with Hermione
- **THEN** nothing in it treats Roopesh's hating flying as something he said

#### Scenario: What the player wrote is remembered
- **GIVEN** a Move said "I tell her I'm going to Shimla for an interview I don't want"
- **WHEN** a later Scene with Meera brings up Shimla
- **THEN** Meera's Replies can draw on what he told her, in his words

#### Scenario: The World moves on around him
- **GIVEN** the World last stood at 7:40 am at Kalka, and a Scene at Barog opens at 9:05 am and ends with Ishaan still on the platform, listening for a bird with Meera
- **WHEN** the World moves on
- **THEN** what happened at Barog happened at 9:05 am, as the Scene says
- **AND** nothing that happened before 9:05 am is known to Ishaan, and nothing has him back on the train
