# Story time told as dates

## Why

Story time is "Day 61 19:00". Agents were shown day numbers with at most a weekday, so nobody knew it was the end of
October, how far off the Halloween troll was in weeks, or which Monday "Monday 3:00 pm" meant. They had to answer in
day numbers too, and a plan or an arrangement could land on the wrong day.

## What Changes

- A Story File gives the date of the Story's first day (`clock.day_one`), in place of its weekday; the weekday
  follows from the date.
- Agents read every time as a full date and answer with dates ("1991-09-14 20:00"); the Director picks Scenes that
  matter from the Stretch's own dates. Day numbers stay the Story's bookkeeping, shown to no agent.
- The Day View says the date. The phone's clock editor sets Day 1's date instead of its weekday (contract 22).
- The Malfoy Problem's first day is Sunday 1 September 1991.

## Impact

host: time, lenses, agents' formats and prompts, views, Story File import; protocol: contract 22; android: the clock
editor and the Story screen.
