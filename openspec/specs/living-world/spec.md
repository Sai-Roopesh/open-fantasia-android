# Living World

## Purpose

The **World** goes on without the player. Everyone lives, at the detail they deserve, and the player meets what
happened only where the Player Character is and only as they would learn it.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Everyone has a Life

Every person followed one by one, and every **Group**, SHALL have a **Life**: where they are, what they are doing,
what they are after. The **Weaver** SHALL move Lives forward whenever Story time passes without the Player
Character there. A Group SHALL have one Life for all its members.

#### Scenario: A Group lives as one
- **GIVEN** Crabbe and Goyle are a Group
- **WHEN** a day passes off-screen
- **THEN** Backstage shows one Life for them together, such as running Draconia's errands

### Requirement: Threads develop off-screen

A **Thread** SHALL move forward while Story time passes, from what people do about it, whether or not the Player
Character is near.

#### Scenario: The dragon egg moves without the player
- **GIVEN** the Thread "the dragon egg in Hagrid's hut" and a week of Story time in which the Player Character never goes near Hagrid
- **THEN** Backstage shows the Thread further along at the end of the week

### Requirement: The background reaches the player only through the Story

What happens off-screen SHALL reach the player only the way it would reach the Player Character: someone mentions
it, a rumour goes round, a letter comes, a Happening appears, or something has changed when they arrive. The Scene
and the Day View SHALL never show what the Player Character does not know.

#### Scenario: Discovering Quirrell
- **GIVEN** Quirrell has spent weeks testing the third-floor wards off-screen
- **WHEN** the Player Character finds the scorched door
- **THEN** the player learns of it there, in the Scene, for the first time

### Requirement: Characters know only what they could know

A person in a Scene SHALL know their backstory, what they lived through, and what they were told or saw, and
nothing else. No Reply SHALL have a character act on something only absent people know.

#### Scenario: Hermione does not know about the egg
- **GIVEN** only Hagrid, Ron and Harry know about the dragon egg
- **WHEN** Hermione is alone with the Player Character
- **THEN** nothing she says or does shows knowledge of the egg

### Requirement: Moments are remembered from one record

A **Moment** SHALL be recorded once, in its original words, with who was there and who knows of it. Everyone who
was there SHALL remember it from that one record, in its original words, when it matters to the Scene they are in.

#### Scenario: Draconia remembers the station
- **GIVEN** the Moment at the Hogsmeade station where the Player Character saved Draconia
- **WHEN** a later Scene brings up the life debt with Draconia
- **THEN** her Replies draw on what was actually said and done at the station

### Requirement: Off-screen time is resolved only as far as it reaches the player

The Weaver SHALL write what happened off-screen only where it will reach the player or change something they will
meet, and SHALL leave a Life that touches nothing the player will meet unchanged.

#### Scenario: Nothing written that no one will meet
- **GIVEN** the Hufflepuff Quidditch Group's Life touches nothing the Player Character will meet this week
- **WHEN** the week passes off-screen
- **THEN** their Life is unchanged

### Requirement: Landmarks arrive on their date

A **Landmark** SHALL happen at its time whether or not the Arcs are ready and wherever the Player Character is.

#### Scenario: The Triwizard comes anyway
- **GIVEN** the Triwizard Tournament is a Landmark in Year 3 and Draconia still hates the Player Character
- **WHEN** Year 3 arrives on the Path
- **THEN** the Tournament begins
