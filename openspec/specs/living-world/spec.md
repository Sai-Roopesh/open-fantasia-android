# Living World

## Purpose

The **World** goes on without the player. Everyone lives, at the detail they deserve, and the player meets what
happened only where the Player Character is and only as they would learn it.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Everyone has a Life

Every person followed one by one, and every **Group**, SHALL have a **Life**: where they are, what they are doing,
what they are after. The **Weaver** SHALL move Lives forward whenever Story time passes without the Player
Character there. A Group SHALL have one Life for all its members.

#### Scenario: A Group lives as one
- **GIVEN** Crabbe and Goyle are a Group
- **WHEN** a day passes off-screen
- **THEN** Backstage shows one Life for them together, such as running Draconia's errands

### Requirement: Threads develop off-screen

A **Thread** SHALL move forward while Story time passes, from what people do about it, whether or not the Player
Character is near.

#### Scenario: The dragon egg moves without the player
- **GIVEN** the Thread "the dragon egg in Hagrid's hut" and a week of Story time in which the Player Character never goes near Hagrid
- **THEN** Backstage shows the Thread further along at the end of the week

### Requirement: The background reaches the player only through the Story

What happens off-screen SHALL reach the player only the way it would reach the Player Character: someone mentions
it, a rumour goes round, a letter comes, a Happening appears, or something has changed when they arrive. The Scene
and the Day View SHALL never show what the Player Character does not know.

#### Scenario: Discovering Quirrell
- **GIVEN** Quirrell has spent weeks testing the third-floor wards off-screen
- **WHEN** the Player Character finds the scorched door
- **THEN** the player learns of it there, in the Scene, for the first time

### Requirement: Characters know only what they could know

A person in a Scene SHALL know their backstory, what they lived through, and what they were told or saw, and
nothing else. No Reply SHALL have a character act on something only absent people know.

#### Scenario: Hermione does not know about the egg
- **GIVEN** only Hagrid, Ron and Harry know about the dragon egg
- **WHEN** Hermione is alone with the Player Character
- **THEN** nothing she says or does shows knowledge of the egg

### Requirement: Moments are remembered from one record

A **Moment** SHALL be recorded once, in its original words, with who was there and who knows of it. Everyone who
was there SHALL remember it from that one record, in its original words, when it matters to the Scene they are in.

Every Scene SHALL be recorded. Part of a record the Story cannot use, such as a Moment whose words are not on the
page or a line far longer than asked, SHALL be left out and noted. The rest SHALL be kept. What the Mac can set right
itself SHALL be set right, not left out, and the agent SHALL not be asked again for it: a person named with their
title is the Story's person, and a mark the record cannot carry is cleared. A Scene whose record could not be made
SHALL not hold up the record of the Scenes after it, and SHALL be recorded later, in order, until it is recorded or
given up after three tries.

#### Scenario: Draconia remembers the station
- **GIVEN** the Moment at the Hogsmeade station where the Player Character saved Draconia
- **WHEN** a later Scene brings up the life debt with Draconia
- **THEN** her Replies draw on what was actually said and done at the station

#### Scenario: One bad part does not lose the Scene
- **GIVEN** a Scene in which Draconia floated in his arms, and the record's line for what he knows of her comes back
  too long twice
- **THEN** that Scene's Moments and summary are kept, and what he knew of her before stays as it was

#### Scenario: A Scene that could not be recorded does not block the rest
- **GIVEN** the record of the library Scene could not be made
- **WHEN** the next Scene ends
- **THEN** it is recorded, and the library Scene is tried again after it, and recorded

#### Scenario: Named with a title
- **GIVEN** the Story has Rolanda Hooch and Minerva McGonagall
- **WHEN** the plan's flying lesson names "Madam Hooch" and "Professor McGonagall"
- **THEN** the Scene that matters has Rolanda Hooch and Minerva McGonagall in it, and nothing is left out or asked
  again

#### Scenario: Set right, not asked again
- **GIVEN** no Lead has a move to make off-screen
- **WHEN** the World's record marks Harry's evening in the common room as his move
- **THEN** it is kept as an ordinary happening, and the World is not asked again

#### Scenario: A line a little long is kept
- **WHEN** the record's turn with Draconia comes back 29 words long, where 25 were asked
- **THEN** it is kept as written

### Requirement: Only the player makes facts about the Player Character

What the **Player Character** said, did, thought or felt SHALL be what the player's Moves and edits say, and nothing
else. A Reply that has someone recall or claim something about the Player Character that no Move said SHALL stay a
line of that Reply: it SHALL never be remembered as something that happened, and no later Scene SHALL treat it as
true. When the **World** moves on, it SHALL move on around the Player Character: what happened in a Scene happened
when the Scene says, and nothing has him anywhere, doing anything or knowing anything off-screen that no Move said.

#### Scenario: An invented past is not remembered
- **GIVEN** a Reply had Hermione say "you told me at breakfast you hate flying", and no Move ever said so
- **WHEN** a later Scene brings up flying with Hermione
- **THEN** nothing in it treats Roopesh's hating flying as something he said

#### Scenario: What the player wrote is remembered
- **GIVEN** a Move said "I tell her I'm going to Shimla for an interview I don't want"
- **WHEN** a later Scene with Meera brings up Shimla
- **THEN** Meera's Replies can draw on what he told her, in his words

#### Scenario: The World moves on around him
- **GIVEN** the World last stood at 7:40 am at Kalka, and a Scene at Barog opens at 9:05 am and ends with Ishaan still on the platform, listening for a bird with Meera
- **WHEN** the World moves on
- **THEN** what happened at Barog happened at 9:05 am, as the Scene says
- **AND** nothing that happened before 9:05 am is known to Ishaan, and nothing has him back on the train

### Requirement: Off-screen time is resolved only as far as it reaches the player

The Weaver SHALL write what happened off-screen only where it will reach the player or change something they will
meet, and SHALL leave a Life that touches nothing the player will meet unchanged. Over a span of days it SHALL move
the **Leads**, the Threads and the Landmarks, write each Lead's off-screen move for the **Stretch**, and let ordinary
days pass without listing them. A span of under an hour with nothing on in it SHALL move nothing.

#### Scenario: Nothing written that no one will meet
- **GIVEN** the Hufflepuff Quidditch Group's Life touches nothing the Player Character will meet this week
- **WHEN** the week passes off-screen
- **THEN** their Life is unchanged

#### Scenario: Five days, told in a few lines
- **WHEN** five days pass between Scenes
- **THEN** what happened lists what reached him or will, with each Lead's move among it, and no ordinary meal or
  lesson is listed

#### Scenario: Twenty minutes with nothing on
- **GIVEN** a Scene ends at 8:00 am, and the next is chosen for 8:20 am with nothing on before it
- **THEN** the World is not moved for those twenty minutes

### Requirement: Landmarks arrive on their date

A **Landmark** SHALL happen at its time whether or not the Arcs are ready and wherever the Player Character is.

#### Scenario: The Triwizard comes anyway
- **GIVEN** the Triwizard Tournament is a Landmark in Year 3 and Draconia still hates the Player Character
- **WHEN** Year 3 arrives on the Path
- **THEN** the Tournament begins

### Requirement: Where things stand between him and each person is kept

For each person the **Player Character** has history with, the Story SHALL keep a **Bond**. It has two parts:
- where things stand between them now, in a few lines, as he has seen it;
- the turns that brought it there, each one line, dated by its Scene.

A Scene that changes what is between them SHALL add a turn and update where things stand. Nobody's secret feelings
SHALL be written into a Bond. Everything that writes a Scene with that person in it SHALL read their Bond.

#### Scenario: A turn in the classroom
- **GIVEN** Draconia's Bond says she bills him for the slap and keeps him on her terms
- **WHEN** a Scene ends in which she floated in his arms and ordered him to put her down, and did not thank him
- **THEN** her Bond has a turn for that Scene, and where things stand says she let him hold her before she
  remembered to refuse it

#### Scenario: A Bond read in a later Scene
- **GIVEN** Draconia's Bond with that turn
- **WHEN** a Scene with her opens a week later
- **THEN** the Writer and the Actor are given where things stand and the latest turns, and her Replies keep to them

### Requirement: An Engagement is recorded where it is made

An **Engagement** SHALL be recorded when it is made: on the page of a **Scene**, in the words of a **Move** or a
**Reply**, or off-screen, in a line of **Meanwhile** that reaches the **Player Character**. It SHALL say when and
where, who will be there, whether he is expected, and whether it repeats each week. One made on the page SHALL be
recorded only with the words it was made in, quoted as they were written, so that no Engagement is invented. A
**Scene** in which an Engagement is moved or called off, in front of him, SHALL record that too. Only what the Player
Character knows SHALL be an Engagement: an arrangement made where he could not hear it is not his.

#### Scenario: Made in a Reply
- **WHEN** a Scene ends in which Draconia says "Wednesdays, in the library, half past seven. You'll be there before
  me." and the player's Move answers "yeah alright i will be there"
- **THEN** a weekly Engagement is recorded for Wednesdays at 7:30 pm in the library, with Draconia, he is expected,
  and it quotes her words and his

#### Scenario: Words that are not on the page
- **WHEN** the record of a Scene gives an Engagement whose quote is not in any Move or Reply of that Scene
- **THEN** that Engagement is left out and noted, and the rest of the record is kept

#### Scenario: Arranged behind his back
- **WHEN** Pansy and Draconia agree, in the Slytherin common room, to meet at the Owlery at six
- **THEN** no Engagement of his is recorded
