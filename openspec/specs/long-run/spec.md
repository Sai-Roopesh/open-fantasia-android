# Long Run

## Purpose

The Story builds toward what the player came for without being dictated by an ending: the **Premise** it serves,
the **Arcs** it builds, the **Style** it is written in and the **Taste** it learns.

## Requirements

### Requirement: The Premise is what the Story serves

The Story SHALL keep returning to its **Premise** without fixating on it.

#### Scenario: The Premise keeps returning
- **GIVEN** the Premise is an enemies-to-lovers romance with Draconia
- **WHEN** ten Scenes are played
- **THEN** several of them move what is between Draconia and the Player Character, and the others are not about her

### Requirement: Arcs are built toward, never resolved on a schedule

Every Scene SHALL move at least one **Arc** a little, and no Arc SHALL be brought to an end because time is passing.
The player SHALL be able to add, rewrite or drop Arcs at any time.

#### Scenario: A slow burn stays slow
- **GIVEN** the Arc "Draconia comes round to Roopesh, slowly, against everything she was raised to be"
- **WHEN** twenty Scenes have passed
- **THEN** she has moved from insults toward slips and jealousy, and has not jumped ahead to declaring anything

### Requirement: The Director shapes the long run around Arcs and Landmarks

The **Director** SHALL shape the Story into stretches that build, peak and rest around the Arcs and Landmarks, and
its plan SHALL be visible only Backstage.

#### Scenario: The plan stays Backstage
- **WHEN** the Director plans the next chapter
- **THEN** its plan appears in Backstage and nowhere in the Scene, the Day View, the World screen or the Journal

### Requirement: Rest is deliberate

After a Scene that peaked, with a confrontation, a revelation or a change that cannot be undone, the Story's next
pick SHALL be a quiet one.

#### Scenario: A quiet pick after a peak
- **GIVEN** the Scene where Draconia chose the Player Character in front of her House just ended
- **THEN** the Story's own pick at the Day View is a quiet Happening

### Requirement: Style is the player's own words

The **Style** SHALL be one piece of writing in the player's words, and everything that writes for the Story SHALL
read the same Style. Reply length (Short, Medium, Full) SHALL be set separately.

#### Scenario: Changing the Style mid-Story
- **WHEN** the player adds "keep the humour dry and British" to the Style
- **THEN** the Replies from then on are written that way

### Requirement: Taste is short, readable and the player's

**Taste** SHALL hold at most ten lines of lasting lessons. The player SHALL be able to read, edit and delete every
line. Everything that writes for the Story SHALL read it.

#### Scenario: Deleting a lesson
- **GIVEN** Taste has the line "keep Ron in the background"
- **WHEN** the player deletes it
- **THEN** later Scenes no longer keep Ron in the background on that account
