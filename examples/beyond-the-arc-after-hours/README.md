# Beyond the Arc — After Hours

A separate, playable continuity prepared from the November 2026 interview. Previous Stories and played Paths are
preserved. Open this title in the same Open Fantasia app as **Beyond the Arc — Rewritten**.

Roopesh and Natalie are twenty-five and have been together since September 2021, before his NBA debut at twenty-one.
He has four championships, four regular-season MVPs and four Finals MVPs with Golden State, and an AI doctorate
in progress at Stanford. His career provides the wider world; ordinary life and the relationship occupy most of
play. The reader chooses whether to spend time on actual basketball.

Natalie is an actress. Julian is her handsome ex and current co-star. Adrian is her engineer brother, and her
family generally welcomes Roopesh. This continuity does not inherit the previous Story's NBA versions of Julian
and Adrian, its family hostility or anything that happened along a played Path.

The opening is upstairs at a fictional cast party, with only Natalie, Julian and Roopesh present. The discovery
establishes a real sexual betrayal shortly after it begins, depicted without graphic sexual detail. It stops at
Natalie's first attempt to reach him. No words, deliberate actions, emotional reaction or relationship decision
are supplied for the player character.

## Relationship foundations

Natalie's love, desire and wanted future with Roopesh are fixed starting truths. Their friendship includes
specific memories and ordinary habits, alongside her wish for marriage and a family. Her betrayal is also real;
it is not retconned into filming or an innocent misunderstanding. Julian still attracts her and has meaningful
history, but there was no ongoing secret affair and he is not her secretly preferred future partner.

Natalie remains active in trying to reach Roopesh. Affection, defensiveness, anger, shame, practical attempts and
desperation are available according to the exchange, with no fixed sequence. The Story explicitly avoids an
automatic retreat into offers of space or passive resignation. Her persistence leaves the player's bodily actions,
acceptance of contact, feelings and relationship decisions open.

Only the three people present know about the discovery. Family, friends, teammates and work do not magically learn
about it. Outside obligations and the film's release campaign supply possible future situations; they do not force
a leak or an interruption. Only the player's End Scene action ends a Scene.

## Basketball baseline

All thirty franchises are real. Real players can enter as relevant. For the Warriors, the names of the first nine
players in the frozen register were checked against the [official opening roster](https://www.nba.com/warriors/news/warriors-announce-2026-27-opening-night-roster-20261019);
the remaining names and coach were checked against the [team roster](https://www.nba.com/warriors/roster).
The roster is deliberately frozen for this alternate continuity rather than following later live news. The Story
adds Roopesh through a fictional supplementary roster slot.

The Warriors' four real titles through 2022 are the historic baseline; see the
[official 2022–23 media guide](https://cdn.nba.com/teams/uploads/sites/1610612744/2022/10/Golden_State_Warriors_2022_23_Media_Guide.pdf).
The four Roopesh-era championships in 2023–26, his awards and statistics are fiction. Curry and Draymond consequently
have eight rings here. Other current teammates do not automatically acquire championships from before they joined.
Natalie, Julian, their film and the party venue are invented; real NBA people are supporting characters in fiction.
