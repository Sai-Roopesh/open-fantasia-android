# Beyond the Arc — Rewritten

A fresh version prepared from the player's 8 November feedback. The original **Beyond the Arc** and its played Paths remain in the same app. This version starts again before the exhibition tips off.

Natalie's family prefers Julian and disapproves of her relationship with Roopesh. At Suns games they back Adrian. At Lakers games they back Julian. Natalie sits with them and joins them, including against Golden State. She understands why this hurts Roopesh and loves him; she still considers those choices hers to make. They have been together for three years, and the disagreement has a history.

The opening puts that allegiance in front of the player. Natalie is cheering Adrian's introduction from the family row. Julian has an easy place there. She tells Roopesh where she will be and stays with them. The game has not started, and his first response is yours.

## What changed

The family's preferences now affect cheering, seats, invitations, dinners, favours and whose account they believe. A great game does not convert them into Roopesh's supporters. Private affection does not automatically repair a public choice or settle an argument.

Natalie, Adrian, Julian, Claire and Daniel have more specific histories and interests. Their relationships reach back through Daniel's injury, Adrian's first contract, Julian's place in the household, the old breakups and three years with Roopesh. Natalie has coursework and career plans; Adrian has professional and foundation problems; Julian has a career to earn and a family he cares about. Their behaviour can be unfair without every action being a calculated insult.

Dialogue guidance now deals with cadence and sentence construction. It allows contractions, hesitation, unfinished remarks, blunt practical answers and the occasional unanswered joke. It cuts the repeated witty professional metaphors, polished relationship speeches and narration explaining each gesture. The family speaks American English. Voice examples are embedded in biographies that the Writer and Actor receive, rather than only in a separate field the current prompt builder omits.

The original basketball premise remains: nineteen, Stanford AI PhD, two MVPs and Finals MVPs, two championships, Curry's protege, and the same real 2026–27 league register. Basketball and personal life retain equal weight.

## Verification

The app's importer and actual Writer/Actor prompt builders are used in a temporary store. Checks cover the roster mappings, all named Cast, every starting rule, family disapproval, game allegiance and the embedded speech examples. Sample scenes use the app's Writer and Actor with the same Codex model as play; they do not add Moves to a saved game. The accompanying review records what was observed in those samples. It is a limited check of the setup and a few scenes, not a guarantee about an entire season.

No shared model setting or other Story is changed. The new Story is named **Beyond the Arc — Rewritten** so it can be opened without losing the original playthrough.
