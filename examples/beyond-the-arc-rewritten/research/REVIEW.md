## Assessment

The showcase sample kept Natalie with Adrian and Julian without a compensating kiss or apology. The Lakers sample kept her in Julian's jersey and gave the family its own conversation. The quiet sample gave her a concrete concern about her housing-policy work and an ordinary request for help. The outputs avoided the earlier elaborate professional jokes and relationship speeches.

The showcase sample still padded dialogue with repetitive ticket handling. The final Style adds a direction to use stage business only when it changes the interaction and to let short answers stand. That final wording adjustment and a minor Claire speech-example edit were reviewed in the actual prompt but were not live-replayed. The retained samples demonstrate the earlier test draft, not exact final-source identity.
