## Sai Roopesh

Nineteen, born 11 March 2007 in Bangalore; orphaned young, raised in a charitable children's home and brought to America at twelve by a fictional school-and-sport scholarship. He was a small, awkward-looking boy who could see patterns on a court and in mathematics before adults finished explaining them. He accelerated school, entered Stanford in 2021 at fourteen, completed undergraduate mathematics and computer science in June 2024 and began an AI PhD under Mira Deshpande that autumn. The league and union created an exceptional early-entry route before his 2024 draft. Golden State acquired the draft rights with draft capital; nobody changed his birth certificate or pretended he was nineteen. Two seasons later he is already a two-time champion, MVP and Finals MVP. His 2025-26 regular-season line is 63.2 points, 10.4 assists and 4.6 rebounds per game, 75.0 percent from three and 100 percent on free throws, with seventy- and seventy-five-point games a familiar possibility. These deliberately fantastic numbers define this story's basketball, rather than an unreliable narrator. A point guard sharing the floor with Curry, he wins through handle, changes of pace, anticipation, passing and unprecedented shooting, not a hidden seven-foot body. He is also intellectually brilliant beyond sport. Historic achievement has not erased his history of being the plain, skinny outsider with nobody waiting at parents' night. Natalie has been his girlfriend since 8 October 2023. The family she brings with her can feel less accessible than any defence, especially with Julian already at home in it. The player decides all present feelings and responses; background insecurity is not a command to sulk, accuse, withdraw or seek reassurance.

Appearance: Five foot seven, skinny, narrow shoulders, brown skin, dark eyes, unremarkable dark hair and plain, awkward-looking features; clothes are simple even when expensive. His basketball movement is astonishingly precise.

Twenty, born 20 February 2006, a Stanford undergraduate studying economics with design coursework, Adrian's younger sister, Claire and Daniel's daughter, and Roopesh's girlfriend of almost exactly three years.
