## What a possession means

There are five players per side. Regulation has four twelve-minute periods; overtime lasts five minutes. Free throws are worth one, shots from inside or on the arc two, and shots legally released from beyond it three. A basket changes the score once and ordinarily gives the other side possession. A made basket followed by a foul free throw is not two separate field goals.

The normal shot clock is twenty-four seconds. An offensive rebound after a shot touches the rim generally gives fourteen. Eight seconds constrain backcourt advancement. Six personal fouls normally disqualify a player with eligible substitutes available. Shooting fouls, quarter team-foul penalty, technicals and flagrants are distinct. Apply the actual situation rather than awarding free throws after every incidental contact or ejecting everyone who argues.

Sources: [NBA scoring and timing](https://official.nba.com/rule-no-5-scoring-and-timing/), [shot clock](https://official.nba.com/rule-no-7-24-second-clock/), [players and substitutions](https://official.nba.com/rule-no-3-players-substitutes-and-coaches/), [fouls and penalties](https://official.nba.com/rule-no-12-fouls-and-penalties/).

Track period, time, score, possession, lineup and meaningful foul trouble. Carry points, assists, rebounds and makes/attempts consistently. An assist belongs to the passer; the points belong to the scorer. A season's shooting percentage does not force every individual game to have that exact percentage. Roopesh's deliberately extraordinary perfect free throws are his own established exception.

## Making the basketball feel lived

The following is the Story's tactical design, not a claim to reproduce a confidential 2026 Warriors playbook:

- Give Curry and Roopesh off-ball as well as on-ball value. A pass can lead to relocation, a cut or screening for a teammate. Roopesh need not dribble in isolation on every possession.
- A post entry can initiate split action: the passer screens for another cutter. Draymond's decisions from the middle and short roll let him matter without becoming a volume scorer.
- Distinguish a screen-and-roll from a screener popping out to shoot. Porzingis's floor spacing and height create different choices from another ball handler.
- A switch changes matchups. A trap commits two defenders and can create a four-on-three elsewhere. Drop coverage protects the interior while conceding other space. Top-locking an off-ball shooter can invite a back cut. Show a cause and response, not a pile of jargon.
- Opponents can adapt intelligently and still be overwhelmed by an unprecedented player. Preserve their actual professional competence; do not quietly lower Roopesh's stated ability just to create suspense.
- Roopesh's height affects sightlines, finishing angles and defensive matchups. His skill overcomes difficulties through play; the narrator does not invent inches or effortless supernatural dunks.

Primary coaching/film references: [USA Basketball: advanced team offence](https://www.usab.com/play/player-resources/skills-drills-players-advanced-offense), [NBA film study explaining split action](https://www.nba.com/news/finals-film-study-celtics-put-it-all-together-in-game-3). The tactical choices above are authored applications to this invented two-star team.
