# Beyond the Arc

A playable Story for the Open Fantasia app containing **The Malfoy Problem** and **Complex Cases**.

Sai Roopesh is nineteen: Bangalore-born orphan, Stanford AI PhD student, Golden State Warrior and Stephen Curry's protege. His first two NBA seasons brought two championships, two MVPs and two Finals MVPs. His third season asks what that public greatness can and cannot give him away from basketball.

Natalie Whitmore has loved him since before the NBA. Their third anniversary is approaching. Her brother Adrian is a fictional Suns star and family provider; her exceptionally handsome ex Julian is a fictional Lakers player, Adrian's friend and still at home among the Whitmores. Natalie understands Roopesh's insecurity, loves him deeply, and still has loyalties and unfinished emotional habits that can hurt him. Their future is not predetermined.

The opening is **3 October 2026, 4:35 p.m. at Chase Center**, twenty-five minutes before a fictional charity exhibition. Natalie is wearing her brother's foundation jersey. Her family is assembling for a photograph, and Julian knows where to stand.

## What is included

- The full real **30-team, 524-player 2026–27 roster register**, embedded in the playable Story's World, not merely kept in a separate document.
- All eighteen players in the verified Warriors roster as named Cast, plus important opponents and coaches: **39 active characters** in total.
- Independent backgrounds, wishes, knowledge and relationships for Natalie, Adrian, Julian, the parents, Curry, Draymond, the agent, a Stanford friend and the PhD adviser.
- Court rules, tactics, possession and score continuity, season structure, travel, media and research commitments.
- An explicit alternate-history ledger: real roster associations are separate from fictional championships and family events.
- A calendar covering the exhibition, anniversary, opening fixtures, ring night and Phoenix All-Star weekend.

Other players already exist in the league register and can enter the active Cast when the Director brings their games forward. They are not all assigned simultaneous individual off-screen plots. The complete source profiles remain available in `research/nba-2026-27-rosters.json` and `research/ROSTERS.md`.

## Real world and deliberate fiction

This is an alternate NBA history, not a report of real 2025 or 2026 results. Golden State's championships, Roopesh's eligibility exception, the added players and the Whitmores are fictional. Real players' dialogue is a fictional portrayal. Invented romantic misconduct belongs to the fictional cast.

The verified roster snapshot was collected on **6 November 2026** and is used as a fixed baseline at the October opening. Later real results, trades and injuries do not overwrite the Story. Extra standard-contract slots for GSW, Phoenix and the Lakers explicitly accommodate the three additions while preserving the verified real rosters. Ordinary on-court NBA rules remain in force.

The Actor matches the current **Complex Cases** choice: Codex `gpt-6.1-sol`, high effort. No other Story or shared model setting is changed. Story setup cannot guarantee a provider will follow every creative instruction.
