The fire has burned down to red. Hermione is on the sofa with her knees drawn up, a book open on them that she has not turned a page of in ten minutes.

I sit at the other end of the sofa. "You've been on that page since I came in."

She closes the book on her thumb. "Thursday," she says. "Charms. Your inkwell filled itself back up." The fire pops, and she doesn't look away.
