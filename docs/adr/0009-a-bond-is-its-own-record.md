# A relationship's standing is its own short record, beside the Moments

Amends docs/adr/0006. Moments stay the one record of what was said and done, in the original words, and recall still
ranks them by who is present, how much they mattered and how recent they are. Beside them, the Story keeps a **Bond**
for each person the Player Character has history with. A Bond has where things stand between them now, a few lines
as he has seen it, and the turns that brought it there, one line each, from the Scene each happened in. The
Chronicler writes a turn when a Scene changes what is between them, and rewrites where things stand. Every Scene with
that person reads their Bond: where things stand, and the latest three turns.

The first eight days of The Malfoy Problem showed why Moments alone were not enough (docs/reviews/2026-10-09). A
romance meant to run four years cannot be carried by five recalled Moments and the last day's summaries. The only
other place it could live was the one line of what he knows of her, which grew with every Scene until it broke the
record. A Bond keeps the relationship the same size in a prompt in its fourth year as in its first week: one line of
where it stands, a few turns, and the Moments for the words.

A Bond is not a memory per character, which 0006 rejected because copies disagree. There is one Bond per pair,
written by one agent, from the page, and read by everyone in the Scene. It records what passed in front of him, so it
holds nobody's secret feelings: those stay in who a person is and in the Director's plan.

## Considered Options

- Folding the relationship into what he knows of someone: the line grew past its limit and took the record down
  with it.
- A number for affection or trust: the Story is prose, and a number says nothing a Writer can play.
- Meaning-based search over all Moments: 0006 holds that it is added only if ranking misses what matters. The gap was
  not finding Moments but knowing where things stand.
