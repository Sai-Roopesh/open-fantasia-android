# Stories are made outside the app, as Story Files

A new Story is made in conversation with Claude, outside the app, and taken in whole as a Story File: its World,
people and Groups, Portraits, Leads, Player Character, Premise, Arcs, Landmarks, Style and opening. The app has no
Story builder. Once a Story exists, everything in it can be edited and added to in the app, and Claude can make
the same edits through commands on the Mac.

Making the Harry Potter Story took about sixty questions over several rounds, research into canon, and curated
images. A builder inside the app would have been a sixth agent and a large set of screens for something done once
per Story, and it would have asked worse questions than a conversation does. The app is for one player who will
not publish it.

## Consequences

- The Story File is a contract between Claude and the app, and is specified as carefully as any screen.
- When authoring a new `story.yaml`, follow [docs/story-authoring.md](../story-authoring.md) so `world`, `people`, `style`, and `brief` are written as vivid fiction notes rather than defensive boilerplate.
- Editing in the app has to cover creating new people, places, Groups, Landmarks and Arcs, because the app is
  where a running Story changes most.
