# The Editor reads every Reply before the player does

A Reply is written whole, checked by the Editor, and only then shown. The Editor reads it against what the Story has
established: its Rules, what each person present knows and believes (docs/adr/0017), the clock, and what only the
player may decide. Small slips (a date, a name, a time) it sets right itself; a real break (a leak, a contradiction,
deciding for the Player Character) goes back to the Actor once with its notes, and if the rewrite still breaks, the
player gets the Reply with a note saying so. The phone shows "Writing…" and then "Checking…" instead of the words
arriving one by one.

The player's earlier app guarded continuity by instruction alone, and this one by hiding what characters do not know;
neither checked the words before the player read them. In The Malfoy Problem the ribbon was "bought in Paris" in one
Scene and "my grandmother's" in another, midnight was narrated and the clock then went back to 10:30 pm, and Hermione
was written as a first-timer after Scenes that said otherwise (docs/research/malfoy-memory-audit.md). The player would
rather wait for a checked Reply than read a wrong one and see it fixed (docs/goal.md, 7).

## Considered Options

- Streaming the Reply and correcting it afterwards. Rejected: the player may already have read the wrong version, and
  words changing under them break the spell.
- Streaming and only flagging problems for a Redo. Rejected: it leaves the fixing to the player.

## Consequences

- A Reply takes longer to appear: the Actor's whole writing time plus a check.
- Replies are no longer streamed to the phone; a Reply arrives whole.
