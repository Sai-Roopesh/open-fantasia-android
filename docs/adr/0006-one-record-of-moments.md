# Memory is one record of Moments, never copies per character

Every Move and Reply stays on the tree word for word. The **Chronicler** marks the **Moments** that matter, above all
those shared with the Player Character, as pointers into those original words, with who was there and who knows of
them. A character remembers by looking up that one record: what they were present for, and what they were told.
Nothing is copied into a memory per character, and nothing is rewritten into a paraphrase. A prompt carries this
Scene word for word, the end of the last, records of the last day's Scenes, chapter summaries for everything older,
and at most a handful of the Moments that matter most to the people present, ranked by who is present, how much
the Moment mattered and how recent it is. Meaning-based search is added only if that ranking is shown to miss what
matters.

The first app added memories as it went, per character and per scene; an hour of play brought hundreds of them and
muddled the context, and copies for each character disagreed with each other. One record cannot disagree with
itself, and a capped recall keeps the prompt the same size an hour in as at the start.
